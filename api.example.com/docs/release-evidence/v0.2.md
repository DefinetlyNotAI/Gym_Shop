## Domain clause audit

### Pricing: PRI-01, PRI-02, PRI-04 basic, PRI-05

- Rule definitions preserve base prices and model schedule, status, percentage/fixed reductions, caps, eligibility scopes, priorities, combinations, limits, budgets, and immutable versions in `0004_v02_commerce.sql` and `src/lib/pricing/service.ts`.
- Quote selection chooses the highest monetary sale, then priority and stable ID; a coupon applies only when both rules permit it; referral allocation applies after coupons. Integer line allocations use deterministic remainder ordering.
- Promotion creation/update/preview and campaign lifecycle live in `src/lib/pricing/admin.ts` and versioned staff routes. Usage reservations are locked and idempotent; quote snapshots retain rule versions, inputs, eligible lines, and allocations on the order.
- Tests cover the canonical 40 JOD, 72 JOD, capped 15 JOD, tie, explanation, schedule/scope, account assignment, deterministic allocation, snapshot, and concurrent global-limit examples.

### Wallet: WAL-01, WAL-02, WAL-03, WAL-04

- Append-only point/wallet entries retain source lots and operation keys. Available, held, pending, lifetime, and provenance are derived without expiry or peer-transfer/top-up features.
- Checkout and payout use shared oldest-first holds so value cannot be double-spent. Capture/release/refund paths retain original provenance; fully wallet-funded orders do not fabricate provider payment.
- Conversion accepts whole 100-point blocks, credits 1 JOD per block, and locks a Monday-to-Sunday `Asia/Amman` quota of five blocks. Idempotency and account locking prevent replay/concurrency bypass; failed validation performs no mutation.
- Verification dynamically controls withdrawal eligibility for every settled source. Revocation removes eligibility. Payout states and dedicated oldest-lot allocations are persisted. Local/test simulation can capture or release those holds; preview/production provider submission remains unavailable until PAY-05 is satisfied.

### Referrals: REF-01, REF-02, REF-03, REF-04

- Random case-insensitive codes are backfilled idempotently; only approved verified accounts may request a valid custom code; historical used codes remain in order/referral records.
- Link/manual entry is visible and removable, replacement requires explicit removal, self-referral is denied, retention is 30 days, and a locked account-level first-order record prevents concurrent qualification.
- The buyer benefit uses shared pricing with the configured 5%/5 JOD/20 JOD rules. The referrer earns 2% of discounted merchandise before fees; wallet tender does not reduce the basis. Reward becomes available only after paid/collected delivery or verified pickup and reverses by ledger reference.
- Customer summaries and staff operations expose status/count/history while avoiding unnecessary referred-customer identity.

### Reviews: REV-01, REV-02, REV-03, REV-04

- Eligibility requires a registered purchaser and delivery at least one day earlier; product/order uniqueness, purchased variant, 1–5 rating, optional fit/quality/comfort, and one-day edit cooldown are enforced server-side.
- The deterministic privacy-safe classifier publishes acceptable content and sends flagged, uncertain, or unavailable results to manual review. Versions, classifier evidence, moderation transitions, and current-version-only Approved badges are preserved; moderators cannot rewrite customer content.
- Product discovery, account history/eligibility/editing, staff queue/history, one undoable helpful vote, and self-vote denial share the same service contracts.
- The first eligible weekly submission awards 10 points under an atomic Monday `Asia/Amman` key regardless of rating or moderation status. Edits, deletion, resubmission, and approval do not re-award.

### Verification and payout: ID-05, PAY-05

- Applications require verified email and phone, enrolled WebAuthn/recovery, public profile/reason/links, and private evidence. Independent staff decisions retain notes/history; rejection starts a six-calendar-month cooldown; revoke/reinstate changes badge, custom-code, and payout eligibility without granting staff access.
- Payout preparation rechecks verification, freeze, recent authentication, and destination ownership. Finance review and provider execution are separate; an actor cannot approve their own request.
- `src/lib/payouts/provider.ts` exposes an explicit `SIMULATION` mode only when `SIM_MODE=1`; configuration already restricts that mode to local/test and refuses inherited external credentials. No APS endpoint, signature, fee, receipt, or real transfer is invented. Non-simulation environments expose `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` and reject before creating a hold or payout mutation.
- The proof-of-concept lifecycle supports requested, review, approved, processing, completed, failed, rejected, cancelled, and unknown reconciliation without blind retry. Completed simulation consumes held funds, failed simulation releases them, and unknown retains them until an explicit reconciliation result.

### Notifications: NOT-03 and NOT-06 core

- Newsletter consent records source/time and supports withdrawal. Campaign audience snapshots do not override a live consent check: the dispatcher suppresses opted-out, cancelled, disabled, or otherwise stale recipients immediately before delivery.
- Exact variant subscriptions are unique and complete atomically only on unavailable-to-available transition, emitting one durable event per subscription. Different variants remain independent. Messaging failure cannot roll back inventory or subscription completion.
- Staff can create, preview, schedule, pause, and cancel basic campaigns and inspect recipient delivery/failure/opt-out totals. Advanced v0.3 retention audiences and reminder automation remain outside this release. No expiring wallet/point notification exists.

### Analytics: ANA-01, ANA-02 core, ANA-03

- Read-only SQL aggregates immutable order snapshots and domain ledgers/events. Finance, Logistics, Support, Delivery, and broad administrators receive server-filtered measure groups; Delivery sees only own statistics; exports use the same scope.
- Revenue, orders, AOV, units, pending orders, stock, support, delivery, payout, referral, wallet/points, COD custody, review, and notification measures include denominator/reconciliation definitions. Driver-held cash is not merchant cash and wallet movement is not revenue.
- Shared date/product/category/collection/referral/zone/status filters feed both the dashboard and CSV/Excel-XML exports. PDF, forecasts, LTV, cost models, and v2 suggestions are not introduced.

## Readiness and verification contract

- `npm run test:acceptance` remains the inherited v0.1 gate.
- `npm run test:acceptance:v02` checks R02-01 through R02-08 file evidence, migrates every schema migration into PGlite, runs the focused unit/integration suite, and fails on any missing software evidence.
- `/api/v1/readiness` preserves v0.1 store readiness and reports `softwareReady`, `payoutSimulationAvailable`, `proofOfConceptReady`, `payoutProviderAvailable`, `activationReady`, and stable blockers for v0.2.
- Expected local/test simulation state: `softwareReady: true`, `payoutSimulationAvailable: true`, `proofOfConceptReady: true`, `payoutProviderAvailable: false`, `activationReady: false`, blocker `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_SIMULATION_ONLY`. Without simulation, the blocker remains `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`.
- Activation requires both the real payout adapter's availability and the explicit `payout.provider: ACTIVE` setting. A migrated PGlite regression demonstrated that the setting alone previously returned a false green light; it now preserves the unavailable-provider blocker and restores the fixture setting after the check.
- Browser simulation exposed and the integrated suite now guards an inherited Finance overview defect: `cash_ledger.occurred_at` is returned under the interface's `created_at` name instead of querying a nonexistent ledger column.

## Professional UI and independent admin workspaces

The admin root is now an overview, not an all-in-one console. Eighteen independent destinations cover analytics, orders, products, inventory, customers, support, delivery zones, promotions, campaigns, referrals, reviews, partner verification, payouts, finance, message templates, audits, staff, and launch settings. Grouped navigation preserves active-page state and collapses into an explicit mobile menu. The role-filtered route contract controls navigation and page-specific reads; unauthorized Finance access by ADMIN returns 404 without loading finance data.

Both UI applications use a single ivory/graphite/sage foundation instead of accumulated override layers. The shared customer header/footer applies across account and checkout routes. Product sections use consistent media-aware cards, catalog filters have persistent labels, and consent controls share a compact responsive grid. Controls, disabled/focus states, warning messages, long IDs, preformatted content, plain list links, and product-detail spacing have explicit layouts. Native checkboxes retain associated 44px label targets. Product media uses existing catalog assets; simulation products without uploaded media retain an honest fallback.

The user explicitly authorized immediate implementation without more design approval. The design follows [Shopify single-purpose navigation guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/navigation), [Carbon's persistent UI shell](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/UI-shell-left-panel/usage/), and [Atlassian semantic design tokens](https://atlassian.design/foundations/tokens/design-tokens/), without adding a design-system runtime dependency.

## Final software and UI closure — 2026-09-21

The final audit migrated the remaining authentication, security, review, verification, campaign, promotion, referral, recovery, upload, simulation and staff-operation consumers to the shared typed failure contract. Every API error now includes a cryptographically random safe diagnostic reference in the JSON envelope and `X-Error-Reference` header; both clients accept only `err_` followed by 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters and expose it only inside the technical disclosure. Unknown server messages and fields remain excluded from presentation.

Fresh in-app browser sweeps rendered 21 customer routes and 22 independent staff workspaces at desktop width and Arabic/RTL 390×844. Every route had a heading, none entered an error boundary, and document scroll width never exceeded client width. The invalid-auth journey retained entered credentials, restored the submit control, localized the persistent toast, exposed its safe diagnostic reference and provided a 44px dismissal control. Delivery validation retained every field. Customer payout presentation remained disabled and fully explained the APS beneficiary-disbursement gap in both languages. Launch settings now distinguishes a ready storefront and verified v0.2 software from unavailable production payout activation.

Final verification passed 69 customer tests, 65 staff tests and 69 API tests; the seven-file v0.2 runner passed 31 tests across 98 migrated tables; inherited v0.1 acceptance, migrations 0001–0004 and the Drizzle schema check passed. ESLint, TypeScript and production builds passed in all three repositories. Package and health versions are `0.2.0`. No local service uses port 3000: storefront, staff and API use 3030, 4000 and 5000 respectively.

The implementable v0.2 software and UI are complete. Local/test payout controls now form an executable proof of concept; production wallet-withdrawal activation is not claimed. APS card collection is not evidence of beneficiary-disbursement capability, and preview/production payout controls remain fail-closed until the required contract, credentials, tariff, callback/status contract and sandbox evidence exist.

English and Arabic layouts were inspected, with the final exhaustive sweep in Arabic. Reduced-motion and logical-direction styles remain explicit. Layout sweeps are not a substitute for provider sandbox evidence or a production-content audit. Generated Next.js development type corruption was cleared and subsequent production builds and type checks passed.

Fresh runtime readiness in simulation returns `softwareReady: true`, no missing software evidence, `payoutSimulationAvailable: true`, `proofOfConceptReady: true`, `payoutProviderAvailable: false`, `activationReady: false`, and `PAYOUT_PROVIDER_SIMULATION_ONLY`. Customer and staff payout pages clearly label simulation and the production boundary. A browser journey requested 5.000 JOD from the 25.000 JOD fixture, placed the hold, approved and completed it in Finance, then showed 20.000 JOD available and the simulation reference in customer history. Desktop and 390x844 Arabic/RTL checks found no horizontal overflow, all tested controls were at least 44px high, and captured warning/error logs were empty. These proof-of-concept checks do **not** mark real APS payout integration or production activation complete.
