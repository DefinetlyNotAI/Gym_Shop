Amazon Payment Services is the selected payment integration. PAY-05 governs verified wallet withdrawals: the published APS purchase/refund APIs do not establish an arbitrary all-source wallet disbursement contract. Completion of payout integration and this release gate requires documented, merchant-approved APS withdrawal capability and sandbox evidence; keep unsupported withdrawals visibly unavailable rather than treating a card refund or merchant settlement as a wallet payout. Work on other important features can proceed.
