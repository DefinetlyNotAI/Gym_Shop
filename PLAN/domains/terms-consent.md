# Terms and consent delivery plan

Planning only: these are contract requirements and draft policy clauses, not a final approved legal agreement. Orders owns monetary snapshots; Delivery owns attempt rules; Privacy owns deletion. Do not duplicate their calculations in legal-page code.

## TER-01 — Versioned agreement

Require explicit unticked T&C acceptance at account onboarding and order submission. Present accessible full terms and a short checkout summary before commitment; record account/order, immutable document version/hash, language, timestamp and affirmative action. Do not require permanent raw IP retention as proof. Material changes require prospective reacceptance; preserve the version governing existing orders. Support/export access must remain available during suspension without forcing new commerce or advertising acceptance.

Provide clear Arabic/English versions as a proposed launch standard. Owner supplies legal business name, registration, address, contact, tax/invoice details and governing-law particulars before publication. Local legal review must validate mandatory rights, exclusions, delivery charges and retained personal-data obligations. Terms cannot promise immunity from chargebacks, theft or mandatory obligations.

## TER-02 — Required clauses and draft checkout wording

| Subject | Concrete drafting requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Price/payment | JOD totals, discounts, shipping/tax and payment method visible before commitment; card and COD only; wallet is tender; no direct points checkout |
| Packing/cancellation | Draft: You may cancel for a full refund before packing. Once your order reaches Packed, customer cancellation is unavailable. Define full refund by actual tender under PAY-04 |
| COD delivery | Draft: Your second delivery attempt is a courtesy retry. If a third attempt is made, a redelivery fee equal to twice your original delivery fee covers the second and third attempts. A third unsuccessful attempt cancels the order. Show D and 2D in money, not only percentages |
| COD exceptions | Resolve free/discounted shipping basis, collected wallet portion, fault/disputed proof and collection authorization before publishing final no-refund wording. Do not charge for an unmade third attempt or imply uncollected cash was paid |
| Prepaid-card absence | Draft: If you are unavailable, we will leave your prepaid-card order at a safe accessible doorstep and notify you. Unsafe/inaccessible delivery is referred to Support. Explain proof photos, privacy and delivery instructions; no COD redelivery fees |
| Damage support | Draft: Report damaged products through in-app Support within seven days of delivery. State evidence request, replacement workflow and refund when replacement is unavailable; substitutions need customer agreement |
| Rewards | Publish 100 points = 1 JOD in whole blocks, 5 JOD weekly conversion limit/reset timezone, no expiry, reward eligibility/timing and reversal rules; referral reward 2% after discounts before fees |
| Withdrawals | Current verification, all settled available sources, Finance approval, published minimum/fees and provider timing; explain frozen/pending/unknown states without promising instant transfer |
| WhatsApp verification | Purpose, resend/rate-limit behavior, delivery limitations and no verification delivery fee |
| Account lifecycle | Restricted support/export page during suspension; fourteen-day pending-deletion identity retention then de-identification; preservation of transaction history and narrowly justified retention exceptions |
| Service conduct | Account security, truthful details/reviews, fraud investigation, suspension reasons, support contact, version updates and non-waivable-rights saving clause; no blanket disclaimer for merchant/driver misconduct |

These draft excerpts need business particulars and review before production; agents must not copy them as final terms without resolving the specified gates. Product support remains the seven-day in-app workflow; no additional claim/escalation feature is introduced.

## TER-03 — Separate consent

T&C acceptance does not enroll customers into all newsletters/ads. Track distinct purpose/channel/text-version/affirmative-choice timestamps and withdrawal. Necessary order/security communications are distinct from marketing. Partner advertising enrollment remains DEC-02's explicit legal/business gate; do not hide compulsory advertising inside T&C or infer permission from verification. Withdrawals suppress queued marketing at send time. Deletion-triggered identity replacement follows PRV-01/02 after fourteen days; consent history keeps minimal de-identified event evidence.

Source: [EDPB lawful processing and consent](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en). This establishes GDPR consent guidance, not a determination that GDPR covers every Jordan customer.
