# Cart, checkout, payments and orders

Owns purchase orchestration and historical order truth. Depends on Identity, Catalog, Inventory, Pricing, Wallet and Delivery. Downstream support/referral/notification behavior uses domain events.

## Requirements

- **ORD-01 — Selected persistent cart:** Quantity/variant editing/removal and per-line selection checkboxes/select-all. Checkout only selected lines; unselected items remain saved, replacing the need for a wishlist. Pricing, stock, coupons/referrals/loyalty and thresholds use selected lines only. Success removes only purchased selected quantities, preserving unselected items/concurrent additions. Cart alone never reserves. Reorder validates current products/prices/stock. At login merge matching variants, preserve unselected state, show stock corrections and request selection confirmation rather than silently buying saved items.
- **ORD-02 — Checkout/snapshots:** Require eligible authenticated account and verified phone; recalculate selected merchandise/discount/referral/fees/tax and shipping/pickup/window. Card via APS and cash on delivery are the only external checkout payment methods; wallet withdrawal capability is a separate PAY-05 contract, not another checkout method. Snapshot SKU/name/options/image/quantity/prices/allocations, rules/campaigns/referral/reward basis, address/contact, delivery consent/window/fee policy and tax/business facts. Separate discounted merchandise, wallet tender, fees, external amount due and confirmed collections; do not double-count wallet as discount. Version terms/consent. [Payments/COD](payments-cod.md) defines confirmation; COD must not require payment before dispatch.
- **ORD-03 — States/payment:** Preserve overall CREATED, CONFIRMED, PROCESSING, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, FAILED; payment UNPAID, PENDING, PAID, FAILED, CANCELLED, PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, REFUNDED; fulfillment UNFULFILLED, PROCESSING, PACKED, SHIPPED, DELIVERED, CANCELLED. COD collected/driver-held/Finance-reconciled state is separate (PAY-02/03). Backend verifies card event authenticity, amount/currency/reference and idempotency. Delivery is distinct from bank settlement/cash handover. Each transition has actors/preconditions/audit; retain failed orders. Fully wallet-funded and zero-value replacements never fabricate provider transactions (PAY-04).
- **ORD-04 — Cancellation/refund/replacement:** Before PACKED, customer may cancel with full refund of amounts actually paid, including order fees; unpaid COD liability is cancelled and wallet funds restored by source. Packing/cancellation atomically compete; after packing customers cannot cancel. Administrative/statutory corrections remain separate. Delivered damage claims have seven-day in-app reporting window. If replacement stock is unavailable, Support may authorize refund without checking alternatives; alternate product/size/color requires customer request/agreement. Finance executes PAY-04; no duplicated quantity/reward/refund. Third COD failure no-refund policy is owned by DEL-03 with prelaunch review, never an invented COD payment.
- **ORD-05 — Views and operations:** Customer-safe timeline, receipt/payment reference and optional later invoice PDF; internal events/notes/risk flags separate from customer delivery notes. Support links, replacement history, payment/stock/audit context. Staff process/pack/dispatch/cancel/replace/note/resend confirmation under permissions. Search/filter order/customer/email/SKU/internal delivery reference/status/payment/fulfillment/date/total/referral/coupon. Record risk review/reason/reviewer; no automatic rejection solely from one weak signal.

## Acceptance

Editing products/addresses/rules cannot change old orders. Browser success without confirmed payment cannot mark paid. Duplicate and concurrent confirmations produce one stock/funds effect. Failed/late payment follows reconciliation policy. Reorder uses current availability and pricing. Customer sees no internal note/risk data. Cancellation and replacement totals reconcile to domain ledgers. Internal tracking replaces external carrier fields.
