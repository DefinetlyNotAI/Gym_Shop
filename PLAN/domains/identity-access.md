# Identity, access and verification

Owns identity, sessions, customer verification, staff authorization and audit visibility. Depends on [shared architecture](../01-architecture.md). Business verification never grants staff privileges.

## Requirements

- **ID-01 — Customer account:** Identity includes unique normalized email, password hash, name, phone, separate email/phone verification, status and lifecycle/login/password-change timestamps. Statuses: PENDING_VERIFICATION, ACTIVE, LOCKED, SUSPENDED, DISABLED, DELETION_PENDING, DELETED. Registration accepts terms and optional marketing consent; email verification limits account activation and phone verification gates checkout. Use single-use, hashed, expiring verification/reset tokens; resend supersedes old tokens and is rate-limited. Authentication modes live within /account.
- **ID-02 — Authentication:** Email/password login with Argon2id; long passwords/password managers; generic auth/reset errors and rate-limited challenges. Secure cookie sessions, device metadata, revocation and recent-auth checks. Verify changed email before switching; revoke other sessions on password change and notify. Staff MFA is mandatory during onboarding, explicitly including CTO, Super Admin and Admin; partner verification requires highest practical phishing-resistant MFA. [Security](security-recovery.md) owns WebAuthn, recovery and session defaults. WhatsApp phone verification is not privileged MFA.
- **ID-03 — Single CTO bootstrap:** Exactly one CTO. Replace the public shared password with the unique, restricted one-time setup-token flow in SEC-01. All normal surfaces remain in maintenance until identity, password, mandatory MFA and recovery setup commit; no default grants operational access. Only CTO creates staff, including bulk preview/validation/conflict handling. New staff initialize credentials and MFA before access. Emergency recovery follows the approved SEC-03 five-step flow; no silent MFA bypass.
- **ID-04 — Staff permissions:** Customer verification stays separate from Delivery Agent, Support Agent, Logistic Staff, Finance Staff, Admin, Super Admin and CTO. [Permission matrix](permissions.md) owns granular class-based, database-configurable grants, including CTO-only staff creation/Super Admin control, refunds, simulation and separation of driver/Finance powers. Disablement immediately revokes sessions but retains attribution. Staff lifecycle includes invitation/initialization, active, suspended and disabled. Configurability cannot remove financial/security invariants.
- **ID-05 — Customer verification:** Separate application with public name, reason, platforms/links, private evidence, reviewer/decision/notes/timestamps; PENDING, UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED plus revocation/reinstatement history. Staff approve notable/partner accounts, never auto-approve follower counts. Rejection enforces six-calendar-month cooldown. Require verified email/phone, phishing-resistant MFA and recovery enrollment (SEC-02). User requires relevant partner newsletters/ads, excluding unnecessary categories; DEC-02 and [Security review](../05-security-review.md) retain this intent and require category/withdrawal/legal determination before enforcing it. Ordinary customer marketing stays optional. Verification enables badge/custom referral code and withdrawal of all available wallet funds, including pre-verification earnings, under WAL-04; never staff access.
- **ID-06 — Profile/lifecycle:** No profile images; minimize unnecessary fields. Saved recipient/contact/address/coordinate/defaults feed orders. Recent auth for sensitive identity/payout/deletion/MFA changes. Suspended customers access only one restricted support/export page; server APIs deny other account actions. Deletion and permanent ban first enter that state for fourteen days with explicit deadlines/warnings, then disable authentication and de-identify required personal data while preserving business history and DELETED_USER_###### references. [Privacy](privacy-retention.md) owns exports, personal-data removal, holds and retained records. The user's final correction supersedes wholesale data deletion.
- **ID-07 — Shared audit access:** One audit page/endpoint for staff; no separate role pages. Record actor/action/target/time, relevant before/after values, reason and request context. Admin receives permitted same/lower-level detail and detail-free locked placeholders for Super Admin/CTO records; Super Admin receives CTO placeholders; CTO receives permitted details. Operational staff receive only domain-relevant authorized records; unrelated records are absent, not placeholders. Sensitive details additionally require authentication refreshed within two hours; stale sessions see a yellow locked re-auth state while normal audits remain usable. Refresh never overrides role restrictions. Permission filtering and sensitive-detail removal happen server-side. PER-04 defines redaction of relevant higher-role operational records.

## Retained profile/session detail

ID-02 supports current/specific/other/all-session sign-out, automatic expiry, reset-token single use and notification on sensitive changes; rate limiting never permanently locks a victim merely because someone attacked their email. ID-06 profile includes name/email/phone/preferences, no profile image and birth date only if needed. Saved shipping/billing addresses retain recipient, phone, country/city/area/street/building/floor/unit/landmark, optional coordinates and independent defaults; Orders preserves required address snapshots subject to PRV-02. Export covers all owned domain histories with private staff content excluded.

## Acceptance

- A verified customer cannot enter staff operations; another customer's IDs do not grant access.
- Public registration cannot create staff/CTO; duplicate bootstrap cannot create a second CTO; reused setup tokens never unlock the platform.
- Checkout fails for an unverified phone; reset/verification tokens cannot be reused.
- Staff disablement revokes access immediately; changed permissions apply server-side.
- Six-month reapplication boundary is enforced; evidence remains private.
- At and around the two-hour freshness boundary, only eligible sensitive details unlock; lower roles cannot retrieve hidden details through responses, exports or simulation.
