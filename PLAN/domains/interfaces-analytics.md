# Storefront, shared administration and analytics

UI composes canonical domain capabilities; it never owns independent stock, money, permission or eligibility logic.

## Storefront requirements

- **UI-01 — Public route map:** / home (hero, featured/new/best-selling products, collections, promotions, category shortcuts, newsletter); /shop (search/filter/sort/pagination); /products/{slug} (gallery, base/effective price, options/stock/quantity/add-to-cart, size guide, material/fit/care/shipping, related products/reviews); /categories/{slug} and /collections/{slug} (description/banner and scoped discovery). No standalone /search.
- **UI-02 — Purchase/account routes:** /cart with selection checkboxes, /checkout with selected lines and visible removable referral code, /checkout/success showing card success or COD acceptance without claiming cash paid. /account auth/overview and existing profile/addresses/orders/detail/settings routes; rewards/conversion/payout/referrals/verification/reviews/notifications as account sections or justified nested routes. Suspended/deletion-pending accounts use only /account/restricted, embedding support and export; backend allowlist enforces this too.
- **UI-03 — Service/content:** /about (brand/story/values); /contact (logged-in tickets, order/damage selection/evidence and general contact information); /faq (products/sizing/orders/payment/shipping/replacements/accounts); /shipping (coverage/prices/ETAs/internal tracking/pickup); /legal (terms, privacy, cookies/preferences, purchase/payment/account terms and data rights); /404 (recovery/search/shop). Content must reflect accepted policies, not invent them. English/Arabic content and RTL flows apply throughout; handle loading/empty/error/permission/stale-data states consistently.

## Shared administration

- **UI-04 — One role-aware application:** Sections: Dashboard, Products, Collections, Inventory, Orders, Logistics, Deliveries, Customers, Support, Finance, Promotions, Referrals, Verification, Reviews, Notifications, Analytics, Audits, Staff, Settings. Navigation/widgets/records/fields/actions follow server permissions. Domain specs own each section's operations. Customer management composes permitted profile/orders/addresses/support/reviews/referrals/verification/wallet/session context, never passwords/hashes. Logistics includes picking/packing/dispatch and replacement blockers; Finance consolidates payments, corrections, wallets, eligible earnings/payout reconciliation. Settings expose validated database-backed business/security configuration at the permitted level. Staff bulk creation and audit UI use ID-03/07.
- **UI-05 — Read-only simulation:** Permission-granted management roles may simulate Operational Staff views and exit at any time; prohibit mutations server-side throughout simulation. CTO additionally simulates Admin, Super Admin and exact users. Super Admin cannot simulate Admin. This is view simulation, not v1 customer impersonation or a way around audit/record restrictions. Proposed presentation must match the simulated subject's allowed view.

## Analytics requirements

- **ANA-01 — Sources/access:** Read-only aggregation from historical order snapshots, payments, stock movements, wallet/point ledgers, referral rewards, delivery/support/review/notification events. No business-state ownership or current-price rewrite of revenue. CTO broadest, Super Admin/Admin permission-scoped broad views; Logistics stock/fulfillment/delivery, Finance financial/rewards/payout, Support support/replacement, Delivery Agent own statistics only. Reports/exports obey the same boundaries; no audit-visibility bypass.
- **ANA-02 — Measures/suggestions:** Preserve sales/AOV/units/category/product trends; inventory value/turnover/low/out/restock/damage; order/fulfillment/cancellation/replacement; driver/zone workload/success/failure/delay; campaign/discount/budget/acquisition; referral/reward; wallet/points issued/redeemed/held/outstanding/withdrawn/adjusted (no expiry); support/claim/quality; review/fit; customer repeat/opt-in and notification metrics. Define denominators and ledger reconciliation. COD collected, driver-held, Finance-received, deposited and disputed are distinct from sales/card settlement; wallet movement is not new revenue. No wishlist feature/metrics; report aggregate saved-unselected/selected-cart versus purchase. Smart suggestions use explainable rules/algorithms over tags, metadata and measured signals (stock, related tags, quality); include evidence, no LLM advice or autonomous changes. Forecasts/LTV/cost models in v2.
- **ANA-03 — Presentation/exports:** Shared filters for date, product/category/collection, group, campaign/referral, zone and status. Initial useful dashboard: revenue/orders/AOV/units, pending orders, low stock, support, delivery, payout queue, referral revenue according to role. CSV/XLSX exports; PDF later. Show stable transaction-derived totals; add precomputed aggregates only for demonstrated need.

## Acceptance

All retained routes have clear owners and appropriate signed-in/permission behavior; Arabic layouts and English equivalents work across purchase/account/staff flows. Interfaces reuse authoritative quotes/states. Simulation cannot mutate or reveal more than the selected subject. Analytics/export totals reconcile to the accepted ledger definitions and never expose another driver's or unauthorized domain's data.
