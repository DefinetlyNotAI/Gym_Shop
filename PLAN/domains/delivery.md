# Internal delivery and pickup

Owns zone quotes, shipments/packages, assignments, attempts and tracking. No carrier integration, external tracking API or third-party shipment webhook is planned.

## Requirements

- **DEL-01 — Admin configuration/quote:** Admin pages configure zones/exclusions/boundaries, fees, free-shipping threshold, ETAs, delivery windows/capacity and pickup locations/hours. Intent: Amman, limited higher-fee surrounding coverage, pickup elsewhere. Do not invent prices/locations; block checkout mode until configured. Snapshot backend quote/settings; edits do not rewrite existing orders. Full recipient/contact/address and Google Maps pin selection with manual fallback. Pickup uses configured location/window and customer PIN completion; cash pickup uses an authorized cashier with the same independent Finance reconciliation.
- **DEL-02 — Dispatch/custody:** Card-confirmed, accepted COD and authorized zero-value orders enter processing/picking/packing. Normally one location/package/shipment through v1. Record internal reference, package contents, zone/window and driver/batch; Logistics-to-driver handover records both actors, time, quantities and cash due. INV-02 owns stock deduction at dispatch. Reassignment preserves package and cash custody with acknowledgment. Queues cover unassigned/assigned/active/failed/rescheduled; manual route order, automatic optimization later.
- **DEL-03 — COD redelivery:** Record every attempt with driver/time/proof/contact effort/reason/result and reschedule history. COD attempt one uses the original delivery fee D. Attempt two is a courtesy retry: no extra fee if delivery ends there. When attempt three is actually made, add 2D once for attempts two and three, regardless of its outcome; total delivery fees then equal 3D. Disclose this policy at checkout and notify the exact amount before third dispatch; a scheduled but unmade attempt cannot trigger the fee. Third failure cancels with no further automatic attempts. Requested no-refund treatment applies only to collected money and needs approved mixed-tender/fault/dispute treatment in DEC-04; uncollected COD is not revenue, a refund or permission to debit wallet/card automatically. Free/discounted shipping fee basis remains an activation gate. Drivers cannot change fees, fabricate debt or mark refunds. Distinguish customer from merchant/driver faults; route contested charges to Support/Finance. Returned packages require warehouse custody and inspection.
- **DEL-04 — PIN/windows/doorstep:** Require selectable configured windows and short-lived attempt-bound customer PIN for attended delivery/pickup; drivers never retrieve secret PIN. COD also needs exact collection receipt (PAY-02). For prepaid-card orders, recipient absence triggers safe leave-at-door plus notification under prominently disclosed checkout delivery terms. Store acceptance/instructions; photo/time/location proof and customer notice replace absent-recipient PIN for an authorized drop. Customer absence then cannot consume COD retry allowances or generate redelivery fees. Unsafe/inaccessible premises or disputed proof go to Support, not fabricated success. COD and wallet-only orders do not silently gain doorstep eligibility. Customer sees own reference/ETA/status, not internal route information.

## Retained operational contract

DEL-02 includes package weight/count, preparer/time, delivery date/window, assignment actor/time and one-or-more package contents. DEL-03 retains separate delivery states UNASSIGNED, ASSIGNED, READY_FOR_DELIVERY, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED, DELIVERY_FAILED, CUSTOMER_UNAVAILABLE, ADDRESS_PROBLEM, RESCHEDULED, CANCELLED; define legal actor/transition matrix distinct from fulfillment. Failure reasons include unavailable customer, wrong address, customer reschedule/refusal, inaccessible location and Other with explanation. Admin/Support may reschedule, driver requests it; record old/new date, next attempt and actor/reason. Customer notifications reflect authoritative transitions. DEL-04 retains driver own today/upcoming stops, call/open-location actions, needed recipient/phone/address/notes/package data only, and optional recipient signature/name as supplemental proof, not a substitute for required controls.

## Acceptance

Unsupported delivery address follows pickup policy. Staff quote equals checkout quote under the same inputs. Driver cannot access another assignment or financial/support history. A successful second attempt retains the failed first attempt. Assignment/status changes are audited; proof is private. Duplicate confirmation does not repeat order/reward effects.

COD example: D = 3 JOD. Success on attempt one or two costs 3 JOD delivery; making attempt three brings delivery fees to 9 JOD total (3 original + 6 redelivery), charged once. Duplicate events do not add another 6. Card-paid safe doorstep completion has no redelivery fee.
