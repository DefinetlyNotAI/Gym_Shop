# Notifications, subscriptions and messaging

Owns one shared asynchronous delivery system. Business domains emit events and remain authoritative for status/eligibility.

## Requirements

- **NOT-01 — Channels/record:** Email and in-site remain standard. WhatsApp is the only phone-verification and phone-message transport. SMS and voice messaging are excluded rather than deferred. Provider details/templates remain prerequisites. Never charge for verification delivery, opening a form or an attacker-triggered resend. WhatsApp business messaging obeys configured purpose/opt-in. Record recipient/event/category/entity/channel/timestamps/status; links use typed references.
- **NOT-02 — Delivery/templates:** Versioned event/channel templates with subject/body, allowlisted variables, enabled flag and edit actor/time. Brand/header/type/action/support/legal footer and marketing unsubscribe where relevant. Queue asynchronously after committed business outcome with durable event handoff. Track queued/sending/sent/delivered-if-supported/failed/bounced/suppressed, failure reason/history. Retry transient failures with increasing delay and finite attempts; stop permanent failures. Dedupe by event, recipient and channel; preserve separate recipients/channels. Messaging failure cannot fail a paid order.
- **NOT-03 — Subscriptions:** Exact-variant back-in-stock dedupe/unsubscribe/completion. Product availability alerts require subscription. Newsletter/price-drop/cart-retention campaigns follow releases and consent. Saved unselected cart lines do not become orders or endless reminder triggers. Partner enrollment intent follows ID-05/DEC-02; ordinary customers may decline.
- **NOT-04 — Preferences/privacy:** Essential security/account/payment/delivery/support/service notices remain separate from marketing. Ordinary advertising is optional. User-requested relevant partner newsletters/ads remains a category/withdrawal/legal review item before enforcement; never ignore an opt-out flag. Never expose passwords/recovery secrets/evidence/internal notes/referral identities. Customer PIN goes through controlled channels, never driver views/logs. Links recheck ownership and restricted-portal access.
- **NOT-05 — Event coverage:** Account creation/verification/change/reset/deletion; login/security/MFA/session/lock; order/payment/processing/packing/cancellation/completion; replacement lifecycle; delivery assignment/schedule/reschedule/readiness/out-for-delivery/delay/failure/completion/PIN; referral qualification/rejection/revocation and reward/credit/adjustment; verification application/review/info request/decision/revocation/restoration; ticket reply/info request/status/reopen/resolution; review eligibility/moderation/reward. Map significant events to channels deliberately, not one email for every internal transition. Staff alerts cover urgent support, applications, low stock, payment/delivery anomalies, referral risk, campaign budgets and messaging failures; drivers get assignment/batch/schedule changes, not marketing.
- **NOT-06 — Scheduled/bulk operations:** Schedule review requests, delivery reminders, coupon expiry, campaign start/end and optional verification reapplication reminders. Recheck relevance, permissions, consent and current entity state at send time. Authorized campaign staff select audience, preview, test, schedule/send and pause/cancel where possible; audiences may include opted-in/new/existing/verified/category/inactive customers. Track recipient/event/channel/template version, result and read history; retention DEC-10. Analytics includes deliveries/failures/bounces/read/opt-out/engagement and subscription/request conversion; email opens are noisy.

## Notification center

NOT-04 also owns the header bell/unread count, paginated history, mark-read/mark-all-read actions and entity navigation. Mandatory versus optional preferences are per category/channel. Restricted users may see necessary notices inside their one portal without gaining other account pages. Delivery failure history exposes only permitted support context.

## Additional required events

Highest-priority CTO recovery requests/attempts/completion alert the preset security email without secrets; cancellation/refund results, COD collection/receipt/shortage/settlement; third-attempt quoted charge and failure escalation; fourteen-day suspension/deletion/permaban deadline, reminder and final de-identification notice. Stop queued marketing at suspension. Describe loss of account/export access and removed personal data accurately; do not say all required transactions disappear. No expiring-point/wallet notices.

## Acceptance

Duplicate domain events produce one notification per intended recipient/channel. Cancelled orders do not receive stale delivery reminders. Marketing opt-out before queued send suppresses it; mandatory security messages remain deliverable. Provider failure retries independently. Back-in-stock watches the exact variant and does not duplicate subscriptions. Notification links still require record authorization.
