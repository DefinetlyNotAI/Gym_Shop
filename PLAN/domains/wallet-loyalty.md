# Wallet, loyalty and payouts

Owns monetary/point ledgers and balance availability. [Referrals](referrals.md) decides reward eligibility; [Orders](orders.md) consumes holds.

## Requirements

- **WAL-01 — Ledgers/provenance:** Permanent CREDIT, DEBIT, HOLD, RELEASE, PAYOUT, REVERSAL, ADJUSTMENT entries with account/amount/source/reference/status/timestamps/reason/actor. Separate pending/held/available and noncurrency points. Retain origin lots for loyalty-converted/referral/review/promotion/refund/compensation/adjustment for refund/audit accounting. No expiry for points or wallet funds. Verification dynamically makes all available settled sources withdrawable, not just referral earnings. No peer transfers, gifting, trading or wallet top-up feature is introduced.
- **WAL-02 — Holds/corrections:** Atomically reserve against checkout/payout double spend. Capture card-order wallet portion on confirmed payment; for accepted COD capture wallet tender and leave remainder cash due, restoring it on eligible cancellation/refund. Full-wallet orders follow PAY-04. Manual adjustments require reason/note/actor/time and protected approvals. Frozen/disputed value is unavailable, not expired. Reverse unused fraud rewards; spent/paid-out recovery goes to Finance review, not invisible negative balances. PER-03 controls approval/execution.
- **WAL-03 — Point conversion:** Points cannot be spent directly at checkout. User-fixed exchange: exactly 100 points per 1 JOD, in whole 100-point blocks; a 250-point balance may convert 200 points to 2 JOD and retain 50. Maximum 5 JOD (500 points) converted per account per Monday–Sunday week, resetting Monday 00:00 Asia/Amman. Atomically debit points, credit wallet, preserve source and consume weekly value quota; concurrent/batched requests and retries cannot bypass it. Insufficient points/quota rejects without partial mutation; explicit smaller requests are allowed. A privileged correction may reverse an erroneous conversion only if its wallet value can be recovered atomically, with reason and independent approval; this is not a customer undo feature. Such reversal restores points and quota only once against its original week; spending/refunding wallet does not restore conversion quota. No checkout percentage cap and no source-based spend restriction. Wallet is tender, not a merchandise discount. Bonuses follow successful paid/collected delivery; refunds reverse corresponding rewards and restore original tender. Points and wallet never expire. Earning defaults are in [Defaults](../04-defaults.md); review rewards have a separate Monday gate. Tiers remain v2.
- **WAL-04 — Payouts:** Verified accounts can withdraw all available settled wallet funds, including every pre-verification source. Losing verification immediately removes all withdrawal eligibility. Pending/held/disputed funds stay unavailable. Finance reviews/approves before a documented, merchant-approved APS beneficiary-transfer submission; recheck verification/freeze/destination at approval and execution. Proposed 25 JOD minimum needs actual withdrawal tariff validation; card-processing fees do not establish withdrawal costs. Strong recent auth/notice/cooldown/ownership checks on destination changes. REQUESTED, UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED, PROCESSING, COMPLETED, FAILED, REJECTED, CANCELLED plus UNKNOWN reconciliation. Definitive failure releases once; unknown transfer must be queried before any retry. Revocation cancels unsent requests; in-flight transfers are reconciled without false balance release. Integration disabled until APS withdrawal capability, contract and sandbox evidence exist (PAY-05); original-card refunds and merchant settlement are not all-source wallet payouts.

## Retained account and administration requirements

WAL-01 retains current available/held/pending balances and lifetime earned/spent/paid-out totals with recent source-linked history. WAL-03 earning rules support product/category/customer eligibility, minimum spend, campaign multipliers/date windows and maximum points/order; scheduled earning promotions may end but earned balances never expire. WAL-04 customer history includes amount, request/completion date, status and safe transfer reference; Finance dashboard includes customer/transaction search, pending/failed/unknown payouts, freezes, adjustments and reconciliation. Account destinations and sensitive operations keep privacy/step-up requirements. Defaults are prospectively configurable, not rewritten into old orders.

## Acceptance

250 points converts to 2 JOD plus 50 points. A sixth JOD conversion in the same week fails, including concurrent requests and repeated operation IDs; Monday opens a new quota without expiring remaining points. Wallet value may pay the full order due regardless of source. A 10 JOD loyalty-origin plus 20 JOD referral-origin balance allows 30 JOD withdrawal only while verified. Concurrent checkout/payout cannot double spend. Conversion and refund/reversal entries balance exactly and retries never repeat value.
