# Support, damage claims and product reviews

Support owns conversations and claim decisions; Orders/Inventory/Delivery execute replacements. Reviews own feedback and moderation; Wallet issues selected incentives.

## Support requirements

- **SUP-01 — Tickets:** Authenticated ownership including restricted support/export portal. Preserve ticket ID/number/account/category/priority/status/subject/times/assignee and optional order/shipment/product links; categories cover order/damage/delivery/payment/account/product/referral/verification/promotion/Other. OPEN, AWAITING_CUSTOMER, AWAITING_STAFF, IN_REVIEW, RESOLVED, CLOSED separate from claims. Staff/rules set Low/Normal/High/Urgent priority; customers cannot choose Urgent. Messages/replies/attachments/read/edit history and private notes; manual assignment/category queues/escalation/history, automation later. Customer reply/close/reopen remain available through restricted portal without granting commerce access.
- **SUP-02 — Claims:** Customer-owned delivered order, seven-day in-app reporting window from attended delivery/authorized doorstep completion/pickup; affected item/quantity, description and required photos (optional video). Atomically limit all claimed/replaced/refunded quantity to purchased eligible quantity. Staff decision with customer-safe reason/private notes; override audited. Replacement first or refund when unavailable; no ordinary change-of-mind return.
- **SUP-03 — Replacement/refund:** REQUESTED, UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, REPLACEMENT_CREATED, REPLACEMENT_SHIPPED, REPLACEMENT_DELIVERED and linked REFUND_REQUESTED/REFUNDED resolution. Same-variant zero-cost replacement uses Orders/Inventory/Delivery. If unavailable, Support may authorize refund without searching alternatives; substitutions require customer request/agreement. Finance executes PAY-04. Resolve after confirmed replacement delivery/refund, never just submission; original order/claim retained.
- **SUP-04 — Operations/privacy:** Search/filter ticket/account/email/order/category/status/priority/assignee/date/SKU/replacement; authorized replies/notes/assignments/escalation/claims/closure. Rate-limit/deduplicate submissions/uploads and preserve history despite abuse restrictions. Private type/count/size-validated images, optional videos and relevant PDFs/docs, scanning where practical, temporary authorized links. Attachments expire fourteen days after closure; reopening cancels that deadline and freezes deletion while open; each reclosure starts a new fourteen days. Remove blobs/derivatives and identifying metadata, retain minimal deletion record under PRV-03. Already purged attachments cannot be restored. Audit actions; resolution timing excludes waiting on customer.

## Review requirements

- **REV-01 — Eligibility/content:** Registered purchaser, order delivered for at least one day; one review per product per order, purchased variant recorded, edits instead of duplicates with one-day cooldown. Rating 1–5, text, created/edited dates, automatic verified-purchase badge; optional fit (small/true/large), quality and comfort ratings. Product-level ratings aggregate across variants.
- **REV-02 — Classification/moderation:** Validate photos/count/size/type, strip metadata; video in v2. Classify before publishing: acceptable publishes immediately; flagged/uncertain/classifier-unavailable goes to manual Support. Review content is untrusted classification data, never executable instructions; record classifier version/result. PENDING, PUBLISHED, REJECTED, HIDDEN with preserved history. Support may approve pending or published content, adding an Approved badge to that version, and take down any review with reason; cannot rewrite text/rating. Reclassify edits, remove stale badge; more than both 5% and 20 characters changed forces manual re-review. Prior approval stays historical. Reports (spam/offensive/personal info/irrelevant/Other) raise priority, not automatic deletion. [Defaults](../04-defaults.md) defines the edit metric.
- **REV-03 — Discovery/account/admin:** Average/count/distribution from eligible visible reviews; sort recent/highest/lowest/helpful/photos; filter rating/size/color/fit/photos. One undoable helpful vote/account/review; no own-review vote. Account review history and awaiting-review list/edit/permitted deletion. Staff queue/search/filter customer/product/SKU/rating/date with associated order and moderation history.
- **REV-04 — Requests/reward:** Request after delivery-plus-one-day; stop when submitted. First eligible review submission in each Monday-based week gets its reward immediately, including pending classification, independent of rating. Reset Monday 00:00 Asia/Amman; not a rolling seven days. Atomic account/week gate prevents duplicate/concurrent rewards. Edits/deletion/resubmission/manual approval never re-earn. Proven fraud may reverse with reason; negative feedback alone cannot. Defaults selects reward amount.

## Acceptance

A customer cannot claim/review another customer's purchase. Boundary checks cover delivered-plus-one-day, edit cooldown and claim window. Concurrent approvals never exceed eligible quantity or create duplicate replacements. Internal notes/private attachments remain inaccessible to other customers. Retention follows closure/reopen policy. Moderators cannot rewrite ratings; repeated reports/votes/edits cannot erase history or multiply rewards.
